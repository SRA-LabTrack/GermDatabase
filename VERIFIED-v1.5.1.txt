GermDatabase v1.5.1 - Photo-first registry cards

- Registry entries now render as responsive cards.
- The first photo attached to any sample under the germ becomes the card header image.
- Photos use the already-compressed WebP media record and lazy-load.
- Entries without photos keep an optional-photo placeholder.
- Card photo updates after local refresh/sync without storing duplicate image data.
- Card summaries are indexed in one pass for performance.
