CaneSprout v2.13.40

PROFILE COLOR PREVIEW
- RHS color visualization chips are now clickable.
- Clicking opens a large modal preview of the recorded color.
- Dual color observations display side-by-side in the preview.
- The original coded value remains visible in the profile.
- Digital colors remain explicitly identified as screen approximations.

EXACT GERMPASM MAP COORDINATES
- Added optional Latitude and Longitude characterization fields.
- Coordinates are stored with the normal characterization record and work with offline/local record handling.
- A complete valid coordinate pair takes priority over place-name approximation.
- Exact pins use satellite-level close zoom and are not marked approximate.
- Invalid or incomplete coordinate values do not break mapping; CaneSprout falls back to the existing station/tested/recommended/origin hierarchy.

FORM VALIDATION
- Latitude range: -90 to 90.
- Longitude range: -180 to 180.
- Decimal values and negative coordinates are accepted.
