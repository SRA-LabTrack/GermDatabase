CaneSprout v2.13.52
Breeding Report Time Ranges

- Adds Single period / Time period range report coverage.
- Multi-year ranges use Start year + End year.
- Multi-year reports can be broken down Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual.
- Every reporting interval is included, including intervals with zero breeding activity.
- Weekly intervals run continuously through year boundaries.
- Adds total interval and active interval metrics.
- Adds an on-screen scrollable period-by-period timeline.
- Downloaded/printed breeding reports include the complete period timeline.
- Technical Report option remains available for both single-period and time-range reports.
