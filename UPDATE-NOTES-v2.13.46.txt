CaneSprout v2.13.46

Profile PDF quality and pagination update

- Save PDF no longer rasterizes the profile through html2canvas.
- PDF text is rendered directly by jsPDF for sharp output at any zoom.
- Profile cards, borders and RHS swatches are vector-drawn.
- Pagination occurs between complete field rows instead of arbitrary screenshot slices.
- Long sections repeat their heading on continuation pages.
- Continuation pages identify the variety and profile type.
- Header metadata spacing is corrected.
- Every PDF receives Page X of Y footers.
- Browser Print also avoids splitting individual field cards where possible.
