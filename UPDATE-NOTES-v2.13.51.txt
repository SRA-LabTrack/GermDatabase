CaneSprout v2.13.51
Breeding Reports

- Adds Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, and Annual breeding reports.
- Available to both users and administrators through Combination Registry.
- Shows dated female × male crosses for the selected reporting period.
- Adds computed counts for crosses, unique pairings, female parents, male parents,
  and active breeding days.
- Adds optional live cloud inclusion for manually registered combinations.
- Adds "Include Technical Report" with objective, methodology, observations/findings,
  and recommendations.
- Printable/downloadable report output.
- Historical workbook records prefer source_date_text when parseable.
