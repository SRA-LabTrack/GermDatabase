CaneSprout v2.13.41
===================

- Replaces the single generic exact-coordinate form pair with an optional coordinate pair for every Origin & Other Attributes field.
- Adds 14 optional characterization traits (7 latitude + 7 longitude).
- Keeps decimal-degree range validation: latitude -90..90, longitude -180..180.
- Adds clear "Optional exact location" form hints.
- Preserves old v2.13.40 general coordinates through map fallback and edit-time migration to Country/Origin coordinates.
- Updates the Germplasm Map resolver so exact per-attribute coordinates override approximate place-name geocoding.
- Keeps a single primary pin per variety using a deterministic exact-coordinate priority.
