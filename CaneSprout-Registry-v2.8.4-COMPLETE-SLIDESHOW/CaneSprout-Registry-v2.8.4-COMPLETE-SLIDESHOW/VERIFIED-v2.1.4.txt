Registry/search behavior
- Registry nav button scrolls to and focuses the search input.
- Default search scope is Variety; alternate indexed scopes: Trial code, Location, Status, All traits & keywords.
- Search starts at 3 characters and is debounced 400 ms.
- Old browse/search cards are cleared immediately when a new search starts.
- Search result pages replace browse results and remain capped at 30.

- no Vercel API/function proxy for ordinary reads/writes
