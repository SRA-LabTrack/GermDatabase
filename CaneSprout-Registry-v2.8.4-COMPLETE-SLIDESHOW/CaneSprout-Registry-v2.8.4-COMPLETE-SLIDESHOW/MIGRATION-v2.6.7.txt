CaneSprout Registry v2.6.7

- Adds a Recently added control inside the Registry search bar.
- Fetches at most 20 lean records ordered newest-first by Appwrite $sequence.
- Recent mode is on-demand only: no polling, no Realtime subscription, and no full-library download.
- Recent results use the same bounded session/memory cache as normal browsing.
- Typing a search automatically exits Recent mode.
- No Appwrite schema migration is required.
