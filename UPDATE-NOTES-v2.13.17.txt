CaneSprout Registry v2.13.17

Dynamic germplasm collection counters

- Accession shows the number of unique nonblank variety identities.
- SRA Developed Varieties counts varieties whose Breeding Institution/Developer/Breeder equals Sugar Regulatory Administration.
- Local Collection counts varieties classified as Local.
- International Collection is calculated as all recorded unique varieties other than the Local collection, so Local + International always equals Accession.
- Counters recalculate locally after manual registration, Excel import/upsert, edits, offline queue changes, reconnect sync, and deletes.
- Historical duplicate observations count once per canonical variety identity.
- Counter refresh adds zero Appwrite reads and uses no polling.
- Shared implementation applies to website and Electron.
