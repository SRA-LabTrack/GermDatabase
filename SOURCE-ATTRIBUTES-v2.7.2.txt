Canonical source workbook: Characterization and other attributes(1).xlsx

A:BW = 75 optional varietal characterization traits.
BI:BJ = Parentage (Female, Male)
BK:BL = Yield Potential (LKg/TC, TC/HA)
BM:BU = Agronomic Characteristics
BV = Reaction Diseases
BW = Tested Location
BX:CA = Photo and Documentation categories:
- Peast and Diseases Incidense
- Growth Stages (Germination/Tillering/Stalk elongation/Maturity)
- Characteristics (Leaf, Staks/buds)
- Overview picture of the variety

The template preserves the terminology and structure supplied by the user. Photo columns are documentation categories; image files themselves are uploaded through CaneSprout so they can be compressed to WebP and stored efficiently.
