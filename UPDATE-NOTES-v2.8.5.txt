CaneSprout Registry v2.8.5

- Background slideshow now always uses proportional full-screen COVER/FILL with a subtle zoom; no letterboxing or contain mode.
- Wallpaper recalculates automatically with viewport/device size.
- Appwrite read timeout increased and idempotent reads retry once before being considered unreachable.
- If Appwrite is genuinely unavailable, the bundled 950-record characterization snapshot is shown read-only instead of an empty registry.
- Refresh automatically returns to live Appwrite records when connectivity is restored.
- Bundled snapshot records cannot be edited/deleted, preventing accidental writes with local fallback IDs.
